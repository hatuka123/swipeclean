# Store listing texts

Limits: app name 30 characters, short description 80, full description 4000.

## English (en-US, default)

**App name**: SwipeClean – Photo Cleaner

**Short description**:
Swipe your gallery clean: right to keep, left to delete, up to move to a folder.

**Full description**:
Your phone is full of photos and videos you no longer need. SwipeClean turns cleaning them up into a
few minutes of swiping.

• Swipe right to keep, left to mark for deletion, up to move to a folder
• Pick a folder (Camera, WhatsApp, Screenshots…) or go through everything
• Photos only, videos only, or both; oldest, newest or largest first
• Nothing is deleted by a swipe: marked items wait in the app's bin until you confirm
• Deleted items go to Android's trash, where you can restore them for about 30 days
• Undo any swipe, and change any decision later
• Items you already reviewed never come back, unless you ask to review a folder again
• Daily cleanup plan: pick a goal and reminder times, keep a streak, see how much space you freed

Private by design: the app has no internet access. Your photos never leave your phone. No ads, no
accounts, no tracking.

• החלקה ימינה לשמור, שמאלה לסמן למחיקה, למעלה להעביר לתיקייה
